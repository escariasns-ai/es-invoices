Number series

Each invoice type has its own series. Tokens: {PREFIX} {DD} {MM} {YY} {YYYY} {FY} (26-27) and {SEQ} or {SEQ:4} for a running number (0001). Changes apply to new invoices only; issued numbers never change.

Each expense becomes its own line at cost, converted to the invoice currency, with TDS off (tick "TDS applies" on a line if it should be deducted). Attached bills (receipts) go out with the invoice email. An expense can be on one invoice only.

Email invoice

"Send" queues it: the mail job emails the PDF from your address within about 10–15 minutes, with a copy to you. "Use my email app" downloads the PDF and opens a ready draft so you can attach it and send it yourself.
